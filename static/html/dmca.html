Copyright & DMCA claims

Qlute beatmaps are uploaded by players. If you own the copyright to something hosted on Qlute (a song, a background image, a video or anything else) and it was uploaded without your permission, you can ask us to take it down. We respect the rights of artists and creators, and we handle valid claims as quickly as we can.

1Filing a claim

Email dmca@jinkku.moe with the following. Claims missing any of these may be delayed while we ask for the rest.

  1. Who you are. Your name, and the company or artist you represent if you're acting on someone else's behalf, plus a mailing address, phone number and email.
  2. The copyrighted work. What's being infringed, for example the song title and artist, or a link to the original artwork.
  3. Where it is on Qlute. The link to each beatmap set or page, like https://qlute.jinkku.moe/beatmapset/1234. One email can list several.
  4. A good faith statement: "I have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law."
  5. An accuracy statement: "The information in this notice is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, I am the owner, or authorized to act on behalf of the owner, of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed."
  6. Your signature. A typed full name works as an electronic signature.

2What happens next

  1. We review every claim by hand. If it's complete, we take the listed beatmap sets down, usually within a few days.
  2. Taken-down sets disappear from the beatmap listing, search and downloads.
  3. We let the uploader know their set was removed because of a copyright claim.
  4. We reply to you once it's done. If anything is unclear, we'll email you back first.

3Counter-notices

If your beatmap was taken down and you believe that was a mistake (for example, you have permission from the artist), you can send a counter-notice to dmca@jinkku.moe with:

  1. Your name, address, phone number, email and Qlute username.
  2. The link to the beatmap set that was removed.
  3. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification.
  4. A statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the courts for your address (or, outside the US, any judicial district where Qlute may be found), and that you'll accept service of process from the person who filed the claim.
  5. Your signature (a typed full name works).

We forward valid counter-notices to the person who filed the original claim. If they don't tell us they've started legal action within 10–14 business days, we may restore the set.

4Repeat infringers

  1. Uploading maps you don't have the rights to breaks the beatmap rules. Accounts that keep receiving valid claims may lose upload access or be restricted.
  2. Please don't file false claims. Knowingly misrepresenting that something infringes (or that it was removed by mistake) can make you liable for damages.
  3. Not a copyright issue? For cheating, harassment or other rule breaks, use the Report button on the player's profile instead.
