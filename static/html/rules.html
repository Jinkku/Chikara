Qlute community rules

Qlute is a small community built around having fun with rhythm games. These rules keep it fair and friendly for everyone. By making an account or playing online you agree to follow them. The Qlute team can act on anything that goes against the spirit of these rules, even if it isn't listed word for word.

Accounts Fair play Conduct & chat Multiplayer Names & profiles Beatmaps Enforcement

1Accounts

  1. One account per person. Multi-accounting (alts, or making a new account to dodge a restriction) isn't allowed. If you lost access to your account, ask the team instead of making a new one.
  2. Don't share accounts. Nobody else should play on your account, and you shouldn't play on someone else's.
  3. Don't buy, sell or trade accounts or ranks.
  4. You're responsible for keeping your password safe. The Qlute team will never ask for it.

2Fair play

  1. No cheating. This includes aim/relax bots, auto-players, timewarp or speed hacks, replay editing, and anything else that plays the game for you or gives you an unfair edge.
  2. No tampering with the game or server. Don't modify the client to submit scores, fake score submissions, or mess with the API.
  3. No farming exploits. If you find a bug that gives free score, pp or rank, report it to the team instead of abusing it.
  4. Play your own scores. Letting someone else set scores on your account (or setting them on theirs) counts as cheating.
  5. Scores that break these rules will be removed, and the account may be restricted.

3Conduct & chat

  1. Be respectful. No harassment, bullying, threats, or targeting other players.
  2. No hate speech. Discrimination based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexuality, disability or similar is not tolerated.
  3. No spam or flooding in chat, comments or anywhere else, including unsolicited advertising.
  4. No NSFW, gore or shocking content in chat, comments, bios or anywhere public.
  5. Don't share other people's personal info (real names, addresses, photos, etc.) without their permission.
  6. Don't impersonate other players or the Qlute team.

4Multiplayer

  1. Don't intentionally ruin lobbies: no griefing, constantly leaving or stalling matches, or kicking people for no reason.
  2. Keep lobby names appropriate. The chat rules above apply to lobbies too.
  3. No match fixing or boosting other players.

5Names & profiles

  1. Usernames, avatars, card borders and bios must not be offensive, sexual, or hateful.
  2. Don't use names that impersonate someone else or the Qlute team.
  3. The team may reset a username, avatar or bio that breaks these rules.

6Beatmaps

  1. Only submit maps you made, or that you have the mapper's permission to upload. Don't re-upload someone else's work as your own.
  2. Map metadata (title, artist, tags, difficulty names and backgrounds) must be accurate and appropriate.
  3. Don't flood submissions with low-effort or duplicate sets.
  4. Comments on beatmaps follow the same conduct rules as chat. Keep feedback constructive.
  5. Content that infringes copyright may be taken down if the rights holder asks.

7Enforcement

  1. Breaking the rules can lead to a warning, score removal, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban, depending on how serious it is and whether it's happened before.
  2. Restricted accounts can still log in, but won't show up on rankings and can't interact with other players.
  3. See someone breaking the rules? Use the Report button on their profile. Please don't take matters into your own hands. False or spam reports may get you restricted.
  4. If you think you were restricted by mistake, contact the Qlute team and explain what happened.
  5. These rules may change over time. Continuing to use Qlute means you accept the current version.

Have fun, play fair, and be nice to each other <3
